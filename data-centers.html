Aerospace components for AI data center projects

Strat Com supplies surplus and hard-to-find aerospace components for the turbine, thermal and drive systems used in AI data center power and cooling projects, with the quick turnaround that project schedules require.

What we supply

Turbine components
Blades, nozzles, compressors and related components for gas turbine and aeroderivative engine assemblies.
Heat management
Coolers, heat exchangers, fans, blowers, thermostats and ducting for cooling and temperature control.
Fluid handling
Pumps, valves, manifolds, hoses, couplings, filters and accumulators for cooling and fuel systems.
Gear boxes and drives
Gear boxes, gears, bearings, seals and power transmission components.
Electrical power
Transformers, circuit breakers, relays, contactors, generators, motors, converters and inverters.
Controls and sensing
Actuators, controllers, sensors, connectors and cable assemblies.
Related hardware
Structural components, fasteners, electronic parts and control panels.

We will work with you to meet your needs, as they arise.

Quick turnaround for project schedules

Data center projects run on short timelines. We search our own spare parts inventory and our network of trusted suppliers around the world, and we respond to each request for quote promptly. Each part is verified against its NSN and part number and is accompanied by a certificate of conformance, and items carry the warranty set out in our quote and sales terms.

To begin, submit the NSN or part number, the quantity required and the date you need delivery. If you do not have a part number, describe the item in the additional information field and we will source it. You may also search the inventory catalog.
