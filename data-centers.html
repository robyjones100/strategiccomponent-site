Continuous-service parts for AI data center power

Strat Com supplies obsolete and hard-to-find components to the aerospace and defense industries. We are extending that supply to the continuous-service and quick-start power equipment that AI data centers require, including converted jet engines and aeroderivative turbines. We supply parts and components, not complete engines or turbines.

Continuous service

A turbine that generates power often runs for long continuous periods, which brings inspection and overhaul intervals around regularly. We supply the wear items and consumables those events require, including seals, bearings, filters, igniters, fuel and fluid components and sensors.

Quick-start power

Aeroderivative units are valued for rapid start and modular installation, which leaves little schedule margin for a missing part. When lead times are long, we search our own spare parts inventory and a network of trusted suppliers around the world for obsolete, discontinued and hard-to-find parts. State the date you need delivery on the request, and we will respond promptly.

Verification and documentation

Each part is verified against its NSN and part number and is accompanied by a certificate of conformance. Items carry the warranty set out in our quote and sales terms, and any documentation beyond the certificate of conformance is stated on each quotation. A mixed list covering engine, gear box, cooling, fluid, electrical and control items may be submitted as one request.

What we supply

Turbine components
Blades, vanes, nozzles, liners, igniters, compressor and turbine components for gas turbine and aeroderivative engine assemblies.
Heat management
Coolers, heat exchangers, fans, blowers, thermostats and ducting for cooling and temperature control.
Fluid handling
Pumps, valves, manifolds, hoses, couplings, filters and accumulators for cooling and fuel systems.
Gear boxes and drives
Gear boxes, gears, bearings, seals, swash plate assemblies and power transmission components.
Electrical power
Transformers, circuit breakers, relays, contactors, generators, motors, converters and inverters.
Controls and sensing
Actuators, controllers, sensors, connectors and cable assemblies.
Related hardware
Structural components, airframe components, fasteners, electronic parts and control panels.

We will work with you to meet your needs, as they arise.
