Quality assurance

The Strat Com sourcing department specializes in the procurement of obsolete and discontinued components. We draw on our own spare parts inventory and a network of trusted suppliers around the world, and we support thousands of OEMs. Requests range from power supplies and industrial computers to radar and sensor equipment, across many classifications.

Verification and quality control

Each part we supply is covered by a warranty of at least 90 days, as set out in our quote and sales terms. Before delivery, every part undergoes in-house verification of its NSN and part number against the Federal Logistics Information Service (FLIS) database, at no charge to the purchaser, together with quality assurance review and signature testing. Each part is accompanied by a certificate of conformance (C of C).

Technical library

Strat Com maintains a private technical library that is among the most extensive in the industry, covering a wide range of aircraft and engines. The library includes prints and drawings, technical orders, overhaul manuals, work scopes, instructional data, Time Compliance Technical Orders (TCTOs) and illustrated parts manuals. It also holds kit information, including cure-date kits, overhaul kits and quick engine change (QEC) kits.

We hold technical support data for McDonnell Douglas and General Electric products, which assists us in resolving engineering questions as they arise, and we maintain resources to remain informed of modifications and new technology.

To request a quotation, submit the NSN or part number.
